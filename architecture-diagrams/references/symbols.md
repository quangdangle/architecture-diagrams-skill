# Symbols, pins and icons

Generated by `tools/make_symbols_doc.py` from `assets/js/symbols.js`, `assets/js/icons.js` and `assets/stencils/`; do not edit by hand.

Use a symbol with `"shape": "<name>"` (an alias works too). Pins are given as NAME (direction) at [x, y]: x and y are fractions of the block box from its top-left corner, so `"toAnchor": [0, 0.72]` puts a wire end on a flip-flop's CLK pin. Directions: `in` in, `out` out, `clk` clock/enable (required once wired), `io` no direction. Pins turn and mirror with `style.direction`, `style.flipH` and `style.flipV`.
